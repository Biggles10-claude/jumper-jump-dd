TL;DR

What it is

Public sale of utility token JUMP on Legion. Price $0.075 → $75M FDV; raise $2M target / $3M hard cap USDC on Ethereum; 4% of 1B supply. Token-only (no Jumper equity).

Traction is real

DefiLlama: ~$25.8B cumulative bridge + ~$12.2B DEX agg (~$38B combined) vs company claim >$41B. Recent bridge ~$0.98B / 30d.

Monetization soft spot

Tracked fees/revenue ~$18k trailing 30d (~$160–170k annualized). ~2.5 bps fee trial reported as planned. Value accrual to JUMP is indicative / under design.

Odds vs Squid

Closest Legion peer Squid (QUID) sold at $45M FDV; later ~$67–95M FDV / ~$9.6M circ. JUMP’s $75M sale FDV is richer, with weaker proven fee capture.

Bottom line

Credible distribution + real spin-out thesis; thin safety margin at $75M vs current on-chain revenue. Main bets: fee adoption, Earn/Perps/RWA, post-TGE float/airdrop. Not financial advice.

Key findings

  1. Sale mechanics are clear on Legion’s help center (primary): dates, USDC/ETH rail, $2M/$3M caps, merit allocation (Legion Score + Jumper XP + waitlist), geo exclusions, EU <150/country (no MiCA white paper), 50% TGE + 4-month vest for public allotment, utility token with no governance.
  2. Independent volume corroborates the scale narrative (DefiLlama), though “#1 by bridging volume / 15%+ share” and 100k MAU remain partly company-asserted.
  3. Corporate story is a neutrality + token-outlet spin-out: LI.FI keeps B2B routing (~$52M equity raised historically; clients incl. major wallets); Jumper becomes consumer front-end with JUMP as sole claimed ownership rail; LI.FI shareholders reportedly convert into locked JUMP (~26%).
  4. Security inheritance risk is material: Jul 2024 LI.FI diamond facet exploit (~$11.6M, infinite approvals, ~153 wallets) hit the same stack Jumper users interact with; team disabled facet and reimbursed.
  5. Demand headlines can mislead: Early press of “~$16M raised / ~8×” conflicts with a $3M hard cap — treat as pledges/oversubscription, not cash raised, until Legion finalizes.

Risks & counter-evidence

  1. Fee flip risk: Free habit → paid (reported ~2.5 bps trial). Users can route elsewhere on same LI.FI rails.
  2. Token–business decoupling: “No equity / JUMP only” is a stated intent, not a guarantee of cashflow to holders; mechanisms TBD / non-binding.
  3. Float / unlock overhang: Community 33% + treasury 22% + investor unlocks after cliffs; public sale is small.
  4. Capital lock until TGE: Funds locked until TGE (target Q4); AiCoin notes longer idle vs some peers.
  5. Smart-contract history: 2022 and Jul 2024 LI.FI incidents; diamond+infinite-approval pattern remains a user-opsec concern.
  6. Regulatory / access: No MiCA white paper → EU per-country offer limits; US/UK banned; AU appears eligible but KYC/local law still apply — not legal advice.
  7. Oversubscription ≠ edge: Merit allocation may shrink your fill; Republic/waitlist preference is not a guaranteed allotment.
  8. Paid research / PR density: Alea commissioned; ct.com press commissioned by Jumper — discount boosterism.
